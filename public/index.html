Frequently Asked Questions

MHT-CET (Maharashtra Common Entrance Test) is the state-level entrance exam used for admission into engineering, pharmacy and related degree courses across Maharashtra. It's based on the HSC (11th & 12th) syllabus in Physics, Chemistry and Maths, and your score directly decides which college and branch you get through CAP rounds.

Ideally, right from the start of Class 11th, since the MHT-CET syllabus overlaps heavily with your HSC board syllabus. Starting early lets you build concepts gradually, attempt more test series, and avoid last-minute pressure in Class 12th when board exams and CET preparation overlap.

Yes. MHT-CET, JEE and the HSC board syllabus share a large common core in Physics, Chemistry and Maths. At Y N Classes, our batches are structured so board, CET and JEE preparation reinforce each other instead of competing for your time — you study once and it counts everywhere.

Maths carries the highest weightage in the PCM group and is often the biggest score differentiator between top rankers. It rewards speed and accuracy built through repeated practice, which is why our program puts extra focus on Maths through weekly tests and dedicated doubt-solving sessions.

We intentionally keep batches small so every student gets individual attention and their doubts are actually addressed in class. Classes are conducted offline at our Sangamner center, supported by regular tests, performance tracking and structured revision material.

You can reach out directly through the "View Admission Details" button on this page, visit us in person at our Sangamner center, or message us on WhatsApp using the contact button in the footer. Our team will guide you through batch options, timings and fees.
